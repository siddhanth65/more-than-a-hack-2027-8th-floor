# Own-projects mining for possession labelling (2026-10-23)

Sources read (code + notes only): `HOME/football-synthesizer` (generator/retrieval/spells_s3.py, spells.py, fp1_shape_poss.py, fp1_kin_touch.py, possession_learned.py, ball.py, docs/tactical/T1_3_DESIGN_2026-10-18.md, results/tactical_l1_poss_e1.json, STATUS.md) and `HOME/analytics-cup-2` (src/runmark/possession.py is only a label builder from SkillCorner events; nothing transferable). Caveat: those metrics are vs SkillCorner truth (starts at the touch), F1 at 7 frames, not Metrica spellF1. Directions transfer; numbers do not.

mtah already ports the touch detector (`mtah/evidence/touches.py` from spells_s3), touch snapping (`mtah/spells/postprocess.py snap_team_boundaries`, snap_w=50), and kink features in `switch.py`/`team_ctrl.py`.

## Ranked ideas

1. **Constant boundary shift (start +5 fr, end -2 fr) fitted on training folds.** T1_3_DESIGN s.1 lines ~108-112. Cause: nearest-player rule opens a possession when the ball enters radius (median 5 fr EARLY at starts, +2 at ends), the truth opens at the touch. Evidence: C0 F1 0.557 -> 0.614, 10/10 matches > 0.55; a snap-to-kink within 8 fr gave only 0.590 and within 12 fr 0.579, so the plain shift beat snapping. mtah: NO shift (grep of mtah/spells and pipeline finds none; only snapping). Plug in: after postprocess, before scoring, shift spell start/end by (ds, de) frames fitted per fold to maximise spellF1 on sim/train matches (also try per-gap-length conditional shifts). Effort: ~1 h. Risk: low; Metrica convention may differ in sign, so fit, do not copy.
2. **Run-edge fallback with learned deltas when no touch is near (S3 stage F).** spells_s3.py `place_start`/`place_end`, S3Params delta_start/delta_end, start_touch_before=4 / after=12, end_touch_after=4. Boundaries sit on touches only if one lies within an asymmetric window (4 before / 12 after the first new-team evidence for starts; 4 after the last old-team evidence for ends); otherwise run edge + fitted delta. mtah: PARTLY (symmetric +-50 fr nearest-touch snap; no asymmetric window, no fallback delta, no end-vs-start distinction). Evidence: start AUC of kinks 0.72, end AUC 0.83, so ends are more kink-detectable than starts. Plug in: postprocess snap stage, split snap_w into start/end windows and add fallback delta. Effort: 2-3 h. Risk: low-medium.
3. **Learned episode acceptance (accept/absorb opponent episodes) with 18 features.** spells_s3.py lines 326-426 (`EPISODE_FEATURES`: n_runs, n_touches, n_players, max_touch_dv, gap_prev/next, followed_by_dead, ball speed in/out, cos_turn, n_other_within3m, n_own_within3m). Evidence: rule-only best cell 0.470 vs learned scorer 0.492 (threshold 0.6), beats rule/baseline in 10/10 folds, held-out 0.491 (design est 0.492, trustworthy); count ratio 0.90-0.92. Recorded caveat: raising a minimum opponent-run hysteresis HURT (0.2 s -> 2.0 s: 0.557 -> 0.330) because real 1-3 s possessions die first; so use a learned/feature gate, not a larger hold. mtah: PARTLY (`spells/merge.py` learned merge and Viterbi switch costs cover similar ground; check that merge features include followed_by_dead, n_other_within3m, ball turn angle cos_turn, gap_prev/next). Effort: 3-4 h feature add. Risk: medium (+0.02 level gain only).
4. **Short-spell recall: one-touch possessions and A-B-A.** T1_3_DESIGN: 25.4% of player possessions are one-touch; 396 team possessions are one-touch only (19.4% of truth boundaries); 330/332 single-touch possessions sit between two possessions of the other team (interception/recovery start, pass/clearance end). Missing them was C0's second-largest error (464). A kink with dv >= 3 m/s is on 90% of one-touch frames vs 27% of random in-possession frames (AUC 0.92). Arithmetic ceiling: fixing all timing near-misses ~0.78, plus all one-touch ~0.85. mtah: PARTLY (A-B-A island handling exists; confirm that a single strong contested-free touch by the minority team (dv >= theta_hi=6) is allowed to create a 1-2 s spell rather than being absorbed by islands/A-B-A fill). Plug in: protect spells carrying a strong kink (theta_hi) from island deletion in postprocess. Effort: 1-2 h. Risk: low-medium (may cost precision).
5. **Dead-ball rules and restart-taker suppression (S3 stages E; spells.py dead_ball/out_of_pitch).** Defaults: out-of-pitch margin 0.5 m on the half-length/half-width rectangle, stationary speed < 0.5 m/s for >= 1.0 s, dead frames never act as boundaries or owner, restart taker's control within taker_window_s=1.0 s after a dead ball and touches right after it are ignored (the spell starts at the reception). Also a same-team restart is not a boundary. Evidence: same-team restart accounted for 598 (nearest rule) / 108 (C0) false cuts, and `rule_accept` auto-accepts an episode followed by a dead ball. mtah: PARTLY (dead GBDT with long-window features exists; the explicit geometric thresholds above and taker suppression are not visible as features). Use as extra dead-stream features/priors for the visible-ball dead recall gap (~50%): `out_margin_dist`, `stationary_run_len`, `time_since_dead_start`. Effort: 2 h. Risk: low.

## Negative / do-not-repeat findings
- Learned CRF with FLIGHT/CONTESTED/OUT/CONTROL states (possession_learned.py) lost to nearest-player: tactical_l1_poss_e1.json noisy m1 0.373 vs 0.575 (crf-minus-nearest -0.20); HMM 0.336. Do not rebuild per-frame learned emissions in that form.
- Raising minimum opponent-control hysteresis hurts (see 3). Snap-to-kink within 8-12 fr is worse than a constant shift (see 1) when applied to a nearest-player baseline.
- Ball-free team-in-possession from team shape (fp1_shape_poss.py, `TEAM_FEATS`: lines, width, length, centroid advance, v_adv, pressure) reached per-event accuracy 0.927 on FOOTPASS (12/12 matches >= 0.80) vs 0.796 for the best control, but only 0.756 on real-ball DFL data; spells F1 0.345-0.40. Possible reuse: a ball-free team-shape model as a SIDE feature stream for team flips inside blackouts (windows 12 and 50 frames, +-1 s advance delta, team-swap augmentation, antisymmetric/symmetric difference/sum features). mtah `team_ctrl` GBDT may already cover shape; check for press/advance-velocity features. Effort: 3-4 h. Risk: medium (a 0.92 -> 0.76 drop when the ball exists suggests only a prior role).
- Ball gap interpolation (ball.py `_interpolate_gaps`, MAX_INTERP_GAP = 8 samples, plus speed gate and re-seeding after long gaps): linear interpolation only for short gaps; STATUS records that camera-cut gaps are uninterpolable and that interpolation brings no real gain in long blackouts. Not useful for Metrica's long blackouts (pathcrf already handles them).
- Viterbi team smoothing with switch penalty in metres on distance emissions (ball.py `_viterbi_possession`, SWITCH_PENALTY_M=1.5): -24% switches; mtah viterbi.py already supersedes it.
